Porto Verde is an original top-down open-world crime game. Raka owes loan shark Dimas $50,000 in the fictional city of Porto Verde. Drive and fight across a 3200x2400 city with traffic, pedestrians, and police with a 0-5 star wanted system, and complete 5 story missions — First Wheels, Delivery Run, Debt Collection, Harbor Heist, and The Big Score — to pay off the debt. On foot and by car, with combat, pickups, HUD, minimap, save/load, and synthesized sound. No Rockstar names, art, or music.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
